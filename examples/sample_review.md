# Interfacial Tension in CO2-Brine Systems: Implications for Geological Storage

The interfacial tension (IFT) between carbon dioxide and formation brine is a first-order control on
capillary sealing capacity and the long-term security of CO2 geological storage. Recent experimental work
shows that nanomaterial additives reshape the rock/CO2/brine wetting state: carbon nanodots modify
carbonate wettability and lower CO2-brine interfacial tension [@sakthivel2024influence]. Because
laboratory measurement across the full pressure-temperature-salinity envelope is costly, machine-learning
surrogates trained on compiled datasets now predict CO2-brine IFT across storage conditions
[@azadivash2026interfacial].
